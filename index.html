How it works

  1. Your agent asks

    It reads our llms.txt, starts a free preview account with one request and deploys your code over MCP or REST: an upload, a git repository, or files written straight from the chat.

  2. It's up

    Within minutes the app answers at its own HTTPS address. Your agent reads its logs, sets its variables, makes its database and rolls back when a release goes wrong.

  3. You keep it

    Your agent gives you a claim link. Confirm your email and the preview becomes yours: a 14-day trial, or the beta plan with an invite.

What it runs

  • Static and PHP sites, PHP 8.2 to 8.4 with Composer.
  • Python 3.11 to 3.13, WSGI and ASGI: Django, Flask, FastAPI.
  • Node 22 and 24: Express, Next.js standalone and the like, built on the server.
  • MariaDB 11.4, its address handed to your app as DATABASE_URL.
  • Your own domains with HTTPS: two DNS records once, then your agent puts names on apps.
  • Releases you can roll back, logs, environment variables, server-sent events, nightly backups on claimed accounts.

Not yet: websockets. An idle Python or Node app sleeps after five minutes and wakes on the next request in a few seconds; PHP and static sites answer at once.
