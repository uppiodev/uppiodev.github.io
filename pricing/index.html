  • Preview accounts are deleted with their apps after 72 hours unless their human claims them.
  • Trial accounts are paused after 14 days: the apps go offline, the data is kept.
  • Beta accounts come with an invite code: join the closed beta. They are free until the public launch and a month after, and we tell you at least 14 days before anything is deleted.
  • Paid plans come with the public launch. Beta testers get a founder’s price.

Every plan has a limit of one CPU core, 1 GB of memory and 20 requests at once per account, and uploads of up to 100 MB. The limits your agent sees are always in get_account.
