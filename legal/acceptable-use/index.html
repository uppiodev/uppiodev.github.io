Acceptable use policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

You may not use Uppio, or let your agent use it, to host or do any of the following:

  • anything illegal in the European Union or in Slovakia;
  • child sexual abuse material, in any form: we remove it, close the account and report it to the authorities;
  • phishing, scams, or pages that pretend to be another company, person or service, on our addresses or on your own domains;
  • malware, or any software meant to break into, damage or watch other people’s systems;
  • spam, or anything that sends bulk email or messages;
  • attacks on other systems: scanning, flooding, brute force, exploiting vulnerabilities;
  • open proxies, VPN or Tor exit services, or anything that hides other people’s traffic;
  • cryptocurrency mining;
  • getting around the limits of your plan, or using so much of the server that others suffer;
  • content that infringes copyright or other rights;
  • content that incites violence or terrorism, or targets people for who they are;
  • collecting or selling personal data without a legal basis.

If an app breaks this policy, we may disable it or suspend the account, as the terms describe. To report abuse, see Report abuse.
