Report abuse

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Uppio is a hosting service under the EU Digital Services Act (DSA).

Contact point

Authorities, users and anyone else can reach us at abuse@uppio.dev, in English or Slovak. This is our single point of contact under Articles 11 and 12 of the DSA.

Report illegal content

Write to abuse@uppio.dev with:

  1. the exact address (URL) of the content;
  2. why you believe it is illegal;
  3. your name and email address (not needed for reports of child sexual abuse material);
  4. a statement that you believe, in good faith, that your report is accurate and complete.

We confirm that we have your report, review it, and tell you what we decided. If we remove content or restrict an account, we give its owner the reasons and the way to contest them. If content suggests a threat to someone’s life or safety, we inform the authorities.

Other abuse

Phishing, malware, spam, attacks or copyright claims: write to the same address with the evidence you have, such as the URL, the times with their time zone, logs or message headers.
